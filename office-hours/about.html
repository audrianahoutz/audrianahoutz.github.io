Office Hours with Non-Mathematicians

Mathematics is often present as structure before it is present as calculation.

What does mathematics look like outside a mathematics classroom? I interview people in economics, science, the humanities, trades, and other professions about the questions that matter in their work. Then I pull out one idea, build a simplified worked example, and reveal the mathematical ideas underneath—without assuming advanced mathematics.
